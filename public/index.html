F1 Strategy Lab

Tyres, pit stops and weather across race weekends. Data from the OpenF1 API, validated with Pydantic, cleaned with pandas, stored in SQLite.

Pace figures are seconds per lap vs the driver's (or field's) race median, with in-laps, out-laps and safety-car laps removed and a simple fuel correction (0.03 s per lap, an assumption). Results show correlation, not proof of cause.
